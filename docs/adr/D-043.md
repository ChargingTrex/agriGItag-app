# D-043: Farmer tap counts start at the chosen event; "Log" is navigation

**Date:** 2026-10-31  
**Status:** Accepted (Architect-Gate ruling 2026-10-31b §4 on M07 review P2-1 and the M08 proposal)

## Context

User Flows promises "Three taps for routine events: choose the event, take the photo, confirm", and "The home
screen shows the farmer's next task as one big card; everything else is one tap away". Counted from the farmer
home, M07 gives: next-task card → event 3 (+1 optional extra choice); Log → event grid → event 4 (+1). A farmer with
two or more active lots adds a field pick on both paths, because the card opens `/log/<event>` without its lot.

## Decision

1. The three taps are counted from choosing the event: the card tap, or the event card in the grid. The "Log" tap
   is navigation ("everything else is one tap away"). The grid stays a separate screen (one job per screen).
2. The routine path is the next-task card and must be 3 (+1) from home, also with several active lots: when the
   task names a lot, the card opens the event on that lot and the field picker is skipped.
3. Out-of-order Yes/No (once per gap) and weak-GPS "Save anyway" are exception paths, not counted.

## Advantages

- No home redesign; the wireframe's one-card home holds.
- The routine reminder-driven path meets the promise for every farmer.

## Compromises

- Browsing to an unprompted event costs 4 (+1) taps, a recorded reading of User Flows rather than its letter.
- A task without a lot still asks for the field when the farmer has several lots.
