The 5xx on `/v1/sync/push` come from two defects:
- [Q04-1](issues/Q04-1-sync-push-entry-db-error-500-poisons-outbox.md): when one entry's insert fails in Postgres (a NUL in a payload string, or an int above int32), the whole push becomes a 500. The entries after it never land, and the retry fails the same way.
- [Q04-2](issues/Q04-2-sync-metrics-unvalidated-500.md): telemetry (`app_version`/`network_type` holding a NUL, `failures`/`photo_upload_ms` above int32) gives a 500 after the entries are already committed.

Under the rubric, a missed benchmark is a FAIL. The two 5xx defects are P2 each. Q04-1 becomes P1 if the outbox blocks on, or drops, an entry that keeps getting 500.
