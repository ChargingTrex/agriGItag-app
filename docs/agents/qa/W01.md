## Observations (no action needed for W01 verdict)

1. **Ties on the same date follow input order.** The two processing events (grading, then packing) are both dated 2026-07-30 with `instance_no` 1. After the events were reversed, they rendered as packing then grading. The dates stay in order, so the acceptance line holds. But the logical order is up to the caller: B10 must send same-day events in stage order, or the sort needs a stage-order key. Evidence: `cmp` in `logs/06-acceptance.txt` (difference at line 344).
2. **The `<title>` is English only** (`Virudhunagar Samba Vathal · FGP8T4033HM7`). The visible `<h1>` is bilingual, which satisfies the acceptance line. W03 (OG tags) may want a Tamil title or OG text.
3. **Developer comments are inlined into the public page** (CSS header comment, JS comments). This costs bytes and is the source of W01-1. Stripping comments when inlining would fix both.
4. **The privacy lint misses known formats.** Its false negatives (rarer phone formats, Tamil labels, see Q00-r1) are recorded in TODO.md:40 and :48 and are not W01 findings.
