## What the test covers

New `apps/portal/src/features/members/PlotMap.test.tsx` (2 tests). `vi.mock('maplibre-gl', …)` swaps in a fake
`MapLibreMap` that records its options, `on`, `addSource`, `addLayer` (with the before-layer id), `getSource`,
`getLayer`, `setLayoutProperty` and `remove`, plus a fake `Marker` recording `setLngLat`. A separate file is
needed because `members.test.tsx` mocks `./PlotMap` for the whole file.

1. **With a GI region:** the map is centred on the pin; `maxBounds` and the OSM source's `bounds` equal
   `plotArea(pin, region, 0.1)` (plan §13 D3); the pin marker sits at `[lon, lat]`; the satellite button is
   disabled until `load`; on `load` the `gi-region` GeoJSON source carries the region and the `gi-outline` line
   layer is added (FR28); the first click adds the satellite raster source/layer under `gi-outline` and shows it,
   the second hides it without adding it again (`aria-pressed`, label); unmount calls `map.remove()`.
2. **Pin only (region `null`):** no `maxBounds`, no tile `bounds`, no source or layer on `load`, and the
   satellite layer goes on top (no before-layer). Nothing throws.

Not asserted because the component does not do it: a plot polygon layer and `fitBounds`. `PlotMap` takes only
the pin and the GI outline (`PlotMapProps`) and limits the view with `maxBounds`; the plot boundary is not drawn.
