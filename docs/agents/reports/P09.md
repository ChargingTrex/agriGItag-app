| 1 (P1) `device_in_use` locks staff out of a shared PC | `authApi.ts` `verifyOtp`: on `ApiError` `device_in_use` remove `DEVICE_ID_KEY` and verify once more with a fresh `deviceId()`; a second 409 is thrown as before | A browser closed without "Sign out" leaves the device row bound to staff A; a new id signs nobody out (B02 rule holds) and the 409 is raised before the OTP is spent | `auth.test.tsx` "retries once with a fresh device id…": MSW answers 409 to the first verify (`server.use(..., { once: true })`), the second carries a new id, which is stored, and the PIN step opens |
