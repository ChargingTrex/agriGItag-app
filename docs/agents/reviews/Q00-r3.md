## 4. Findings

### P1 (blocks the merge)

**P1-1 A land-record number between 1900 and 2099, or in a date-like shape, passes even after an explicit "No.". This is a regression from this round's P2-1 fix.**
- *What the code does:* the land-record rule (`privacy-lint.py`, the first `re.compile` after the `<th>` rule) makes `No.`/`Number`/`num`/`எண்` optional. It then applies `(?!{NOT_A_NUMBER})` to whatever number follows. `NOT_A_NUMBER` matches:
  - `(?:19|20)\d\d(?!\d)`;
  - `\d{1,2}[./-]\d{1,2}[./-]\d{2,4}`;
  - `\d{1,2}\s*(?:jan|…|dec)[a-z]*\b`.
- *What goes wrong:* the guard is right after a bare `survey` (`Pest survey 2026`). It is wrong after `No.`/`Number`/`எண்`, which say that a land-record number follows. The cases that slip:
  - `Patta No. 1987`, `S.F. No. 2015/3A`, `Survey No. 2041/2` and `சர்வே எண் 2008/1` (R10–R13);
  - `Sy. No. 12/3/45` (R16);
  - `Survey No. 45 Market Road` and `Patta No. 3 Mariamman Koil Street` (R15, R14). The month alternation's `[a-z]*` lets "Market", "Mariamman" and "Mayiladuthurai" count as months.

  All pass with exit 0. The round-2 linter caught all of them except R14.
- *Why it matters:* patta numbers are issued in sequence per village and commonly run into the thousands, and large villages have 4-digit survey numbers. A real slice of land records therefore uses exactly the round-2 format and slips. CLAUDE.md §6 and BM-09 rely on this rule; nothing else catches a survey number.
- *How to fix (tested, see §1):* split the land-record rule in two, and tighten the month list:
  ```python
  # explicit "No." / "Number" / "எண்": any number counts
  re.compile(
      r"(?P<w>\b(?:survey|patta|chitta|S\.?\s?F\.?|Sy\.?)\s*(?:no\b\.?|number|num\b\.?)|(?:சர்வே|பட்டா|சிட்டா|புல)\s*எண்)"
      r"(?=\s*[:#.\-]?\s*\d)",
      re.IGNORECASE,
  ),
  # bare label: the year/date guard applies
  re.compile(
      r"(?P<w>\b(?:survey|patta|chitta|S\.?\s?F\.?|Sy\.?)|(?:சர்வே|பட்டா|சிட்டா))"
      rf"(?=\s*[:#.\-]?\s*(?!{NOT_A_NUMBER})\d+(?:/[0-9A-Za-z]+)?)",
      re.IGNORECASE,
  ),
  # and in NOT_A_NUMBER, whole month names only:
  r"|\d{1,2}\s*(?:jan(?:uary)?|feb(?:ruary)?|mar(?:ch)?|apr(?:il)?|may|june?|july?|aug(?:ust)?|sep(?:t(?:ember)?)?|oct(?:ober)?|nov(?:ember)?|dec(?:ember)?)\b"
  ```
  With this change, R10–R16 are caught and the other 83 probes are unchanged. That includes p3/1–4, G01–G04 and `Sy. No. of plots: 3`. Both fixtures give the same counts (0 and 62), and the suite gives `68 passed`. Add R10, R11, R13, R15 and R16 to `LEAKS`.
- *If skipped:* a farmer's patta number in the 1900–2099 range is printed on a public page while BM-09 stays green.
