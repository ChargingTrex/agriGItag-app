   §13 D1 resolved the conflict between the source documents' 30 MB and the user's 100 MB. There, 30 MB is the
   **download target** (the device-specific AAB size, `bundletool get-size total`), and 100 MB is the **hard ceiling on
   the universal APK** (BM-04: `apk_arm64_mb ≤ 100`, `download_mb ≤ 30`). D-002 records the same, with a 40 MB APK
   target. The §3 wording is the source document's older figure.
   - The arm64 APK at **30.03 MiB** (31,483,764 bytes, `docs/agents/qa/M13.md:7`) **meets** the ceiling and the
     40 MB target.
